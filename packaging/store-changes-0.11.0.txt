Kinetic Compare: lay two revisions of a drawing set over each other.

- Tools > Kinetic Compare opens another revision beside the one you have open, in three columns: the original, the compared set, and the two laid over each other - red where only the original draws, blue where only the compared set does, dark where both do.
- Pair the sheets up by dragging them into place, putting blank sheets in, or leaving sheets out. Neither file is changed.
- Move around freely: drag with the middle button, scroll with the wheel, and Ctrl+wheel zooms on the sheet under the pointer.
- Generate writes the overlay as a PDF, in vectors, and opens it in a new window, ready to mark up and measure like any other. Each revision is a layer of its own that other PDF viewers can turn off.
- In a generated overlay, a slider fades from the original to the compared set.
- With the slider at either end, Clip, Cut and Erase work on that revision alone.
