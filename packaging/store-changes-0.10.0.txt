Text boxes, clipping, and turning and stretching markups.

- Type text boxes on the page, with or without a pointing arrow, in any installed font. Style them a character at a time (font, size, colour, bold, italic, underline) and set alignment, padding, border, background and fit-to-box.
- Text boxes in a PDF whose font isn't installed on this PC now show in the font the file carries.
- Clip an area of a page, with the markups over it, and paste it as a movable, resizable markup in any open document.
- Cut lifts the drawing out of an area; Erase removes the drawing in an area when the file is saved.
- Copy and paste measurements, clips and markups with Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V, in place or under the pointer.
- Blank sheets are now ordinary pages of the document, so every tool works on them straight away.
- Selected markups get a frame to stretch them by, and handles to turn them, together as one step to undo.
- Faster drawing of documents with many text boxes.
- The app now tells you when a newer version is out, even while it stays open, and can install it from the toolbar.
- After an update, a short "What's new" shows what changed. It is also in the File menu.
