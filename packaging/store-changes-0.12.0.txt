Everything since the last Store release, 0.10.0: Kinetic Compare, and layers.

Kinetic Compare: lay two revisions of a drawing set over each other.

- Tools > Kinetic Compare opens another revision beside the one you have open, in three columns: the original, the compared set, and the two laid over each other - red where only the original draws, blue where only the compared set does, dark where both do.
- Pair the sheets up by dragging them into place, putting blank sheets in, or leaving sheets out. Neither file is changed.
- Move around freely: drag with the middle button, scroll with the wheel, and Ctrl+wheel zooms on the sheet under the pointer.
- Generate writes the overlay as a PDF, in vectors, and opens it in a new window, ready to mark up and measure like any other. Each revision is a layer of its own that other PDF viewers can turn off.
- In a generated overlay, a slider fades from the original to the compared set.
- With the slider at either end, Clip, Cut and Erase work on that revision alone.

Layers: name your markups, nest them, and order them back to front.

- The Layers panel holds a named layer for each set of markups, with its own visibility, lock and label colour. Drag a layer to reorder or nest it, fold it away, rename it, or click it to draw on it.
- Right-click a markup to bring it to front, send it to back, move it forward or backward, or move it to another layer - including a new one made on the spot.
- A hidden or locked layer can't be picked by any route, so it stays out of the way while you work.
- Layers are written as the PDF's own optional content groups, so other viewers can turn them on and off too.
- The Rectangle, Ellipse, Line, Arrow and Pen tools now save as standard PDF annotations, and take part in layers like everything else.
- Zoomed out past 20%, the sheet view draws every markup over the thumbnails.

Faster on dense drawings:

- Zooming and panning a heavy sheet is drawn to a budget fitted to what your GPU really takes, instead of a fixed guess - a median step of 459 ms became 58 ms.
- Scrolling no longer jumps backwards on laptops with two GPUs: frames are paced by the app from the compositor's timing.
- Hybrid laptops ask for the discrete GPU again, which v0.9.3 had dropped.
- A page is no longer read again for images that would barely grow, or reloaded over and over when a sharper read is asked for.
