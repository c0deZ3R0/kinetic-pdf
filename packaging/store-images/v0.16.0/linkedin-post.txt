I thought I couldn’t make Kinetic PDF faster. Turns out, there was more room to improve.

So here’s a challenge: take your slowest, most detailed PDF, open it in Kinetic PDF v0.16.0, and let it finish caching.

Then zoom and pan as fast as you can. Jump between details. Go back over areas you’ve already visited.

Can you get ahead of the rendering?

That’s what I’ve been working on—less waiting while moving around big drawings. If you manage to make it struggle, I’d like to know where.
