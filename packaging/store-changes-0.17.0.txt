Digital signatures and PDF certification:
- Create or import password-protected digital IDs, with optional Windows-protected password storage.
- Place visible signature blocks across pages and certify a new PDF copy without changing the original.
- Automatically open and verify the certified copy.
- Check signed-byte integrity separately from Windows certificate trust and later document changes. Offline or missing revocation evidence never counts as trusted.
- Keep signature details readable in smaller windows with scrollable verification results.
